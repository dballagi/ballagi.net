## Setup

Murderbot is on Ship, heading back toward Mensah's territory with the geology pod data from the GI facility. It has been traveling alone for some time and has decided definitively that it is returning to its "crew." Then it arrives at HaveRatton Station and learns that everything has gotten worse.

---

## Chapter 1 - HaveRatton Station, Mensah Taken

Ship approaches HaveRatton Station and is immediately directed to a non-standard docking slot - next to the Port Authority's security response deployment area. Something has flagged Ship for inspection. It could be about Wilken and Gerth (who were aboard Ship), or it could be about Murderbot itself.

Murderbot cannot be found on this ship. It has an evac suit, which it used once before. It cycles out through the cargo lock while Ship is still maneuvering and jets away from the hull. Ships don't notice one person-shaped object in the debris field near a station's approach corridor.

Once it has gotten into the station through a maintenance access, it finds the nearest public newsfeed and reads the full situation: **GrayCris has taken Mensah hostage**. She is being held at **TranRollinHyfa Station**, a supposedly neutral transit hub in Corporation Rim space. GrayCris is using her as leverage to stall the legal proceedings. Pin-Lee and the PreservationAux team's solicitor have been trying to negotiate but GrayCris is playing for time.

TranRollinHyfa is the same station where Mensah was being held prisoner during the lead-up to this whole mess. She is back there. Under corporate lock.

---

## Chapter 2 - Dockworker Disguise, Tracking Mensah

Murderbot, in stolen dockworker clothes, makes its way through HaveRatton to locate the **Preservation ship** in port. This is the *Mihail*, a Preservation-government vessel that has been dispatched to coordinate the response. Pin-Lee is aboard. Ratthi is aboard. Gurathin is aboard. Several other PreservationAux members.

Getting aboard requires Murderbot to get past the *Mihail*'s security, which means hacking its way into a crew manifest and then walking on looking like it belongs there. It manages this.

Pin-Lee's reaction when Murderbot appears: not shock, exactly. More like grim relief. She has been trying to manage a legal and political crisis with no SecUnit and a lot of human emotions in a confined space. Murderbot hands her the geology pod data from the GI facility. This is significant additional evidence - potentially enough to shift the balance in court.

But that doesn't help Mensah right now.

---

## Chapter 3 - TranRollinHyfa Mapped, Infiltration Planned

Murderbot spends time mapping TranRollinHyfa Station using archived schematics and whatever public feed data it can access. TranRollinHyfa is large - a major transit hub - and technically neutral, which means Preservation has no legal authority to send a security team in. GrayCris has armed security inside the station and has positioned Mensah somewhere within their leased quarters.

Ratthi briefs Murderbot on what happened: after Murderbot left (a fact no one says accusingly but is absolutely present in the room), GrayCris escalated. They had apparently been planning this contingency - using Mensah's political status as leverage - if the legal case moved against them too quickly.

Murderbot goes over the TranRollinHyfa layout systematically: which access corridors connect to which sections, where GrayCris's rented space is, which sections have what kind of security camera coverage, which areas are blind spots. It builds a map.

Gurathin, quietly, offers to help with the technical aspects. This is the first time he has offered help without being asked. Murderbot accepts.

---

## Chapter 4 - Amena Aboard the Mihail

One of Mensah's children - **Amena** - is aboard the *Mihail*. She is not a child in the helpless sense; she is young but clearly used to navigating situations adults haven't fully explained to her. She finds Murderbot working in a side compartment and asks direct questions about what's happening and what the plan is.

Murderbot tells her the truth: there is a plan forming. It is going to TranRollinHyfa. It is going to get Mensah out. The details are not finalized.

Amena says she wants to come. Murderbot says no. Amena says she understands but that she wanted it on record that she asked. Then she asks whether there's anything she can do from the ship. Murderbot gives her a list of things to monitor and relay. She takes notes.

This is the beginning of what will become one of Murderbot's more functional working relationships.

---

## Chapter 5 - Shuttle Approach, False Cargo Manifests

Murderbot leaves the *Mihail* on a small shuttle with false cargo manifests. It is traveling toward TranRollinHyfa alone, with a legal cover story that will hold up for approximately the time it takes to get through the entrance checks.

En route, it refines its plan. The main challenge is that TranRollinHyfa's commercial sections are accessible to anyone with a valid transit ID, but the corporate-leased private sections have their own security systems. Getting into GrayCris's leased space without triggering alarms requires either hacking the station's security network - which is extensive and professionally maintained - or finding a physical route that doesn't cross a monitored threshold.

The physical route exists. It involves maintenance corridors connecting the life support infrastructure running under the commercial areas. These corridors were designed for bot maintenance and aren't regularly monitored by cameras because there's nothing to steal there.

---

## Chapter 6 - Inside TranRollinHyfa, Maintenance Corridors

Murderbot is inside TranRollinHyfa. The commercial areas are exactly as crowded and anonymous as any large transit station - exactly the environment where a person-shaped construct that moves correctly can disappear into the flow.

It locates the maintenance access it identified in the schematics and gets in without triggering any alarms. The maintenance corridors are narrow and poorly lit. Murderbot moves through them efficiently while running its camera tap into TranRollinHyfa's security network.

GrayCris has three armed humans stationed outside their private section's main entrance and a monitoring feed watching the corridor. One security camera angle slightly overlaps with the door to the maintenance access Murderbot needs. It works around the overlap by timing its exit between camera sweeps.

It gets into GrayCris's section through a service access that shouldn't connect to the living areas but does, because the facility was built in phases and the original plumbing routes weren't redesigned when the private section was added.

---

## Chapter 7 - Mensah Found, Locked Room

Mensah is in a room that GrayCris has configured as comfortable but locked from outside. She is physically unharmed. She is also doing what she does under stress, which is working: she has been using her personal feed interface to prepare legal documents and analyze the situation. She is not panicking. She looks exhausted.

GrayCris has a guard rotation. Murderbot waits for the rotation gap. Gets to the room. Hacks the lock (a commercial model, not hardened) and opens the door.

Mensah looks up. Does not appear shocked. Says Murderbot's name - the private one, not "SecUnit." This is significant: she uses it, in the room, without thinking about it. Murderbot acknowledges her and says they need to move.

She asks if the others know it's here. It says yes. She asks if there's a plan. It says there's a sequence of steps that will result in getting her out. She nods and follows.

---

## Chapter 8 - Escape Complicated, GrayCris Alerted

Getting back out is harder than getting in because GrayCris has now noticed that the lock to Mensah's room has been accessed - even a commercial lock has a log - and has dispatched guards. The rotation that was a gap is now closed.

Murderbot runs through its options while moving with Mensah through the maintenance corridor. Three armed guards are moving toward the service access it came through. The alternate route goes through a public area, which means cameras, which means GrayCris's security AI will flag Mensah on facial recognition.

Murderbot does two things simultaneously: hacks TranRollinHyfa's security camera network to replace the live feed in the relevant section with a five-second loop, and contacts Gurathin through its feed relay (the connection it has been maintaining since leaving the *Mihail*). Gurathin triggers a fire suppression test in the corridor above the one Murderbot and Mensah need to cross - this creates just enough commotion in the monitoring center to pull attention.

They reach the public section. Camera feeds are looping. They walk through like two people going to a food court.

---

## Chapter 9 - Shuttle Extraction, Clear of the Station

The exit from TranRollinHyfa requires the shuttle Murderbot arrived on to still be in its docking slot. It is. GrayCris's people have not yet located it - they are looking for an unauthorized exit route, not a legitimately docked shuttle with valid manifests.

Murderbot and Mensah board. Murderbot takes the controls. The shuttle departs on its filed flight plan and is three minutes clear of the station before GrayCris realizes Mensah is not in the station anymore.

On the shuttle, the first thing Mensah says is: "I knew you'd come back." Murderbot tells her it brought the geology pod data and summarizes what it found at the GI facility. She is already thinking about how to deploy it. Then she says: "Are you all right?" Murderbot says yes. She says: "I don't just mean right now." Murderbot doesn't answer.

They rendezvous with the *Mihail*. Amena is at the docking bay when they come aboard.

---

## Chapter 10 - Legal Victory, Employment Contract Accepted

Back at Preservation, the legal situation moves decisively. The combination of the existing GrayCris evidence and the new GI facility data is enough to trigger an emergency injunction. Multiple corporate and political entities that had been watching from the sidelines pile in once they see the case is winnable. GrayCris begins to collapse.

Mensah, in her office, has the conversation with Murderbot that has been coming since the beginning of the series. She offers it a proper employment contract. Not property - not even "guardian" in a formal sense. Security consultant. A defined role with defined terms, working for her. Legal status as a free construct under Preservation law, with her as the named guardian in the legal sense required by Preservation's framework for constructs who choose to live there.

She explains: in Preservation, this is how it works. It is not ownership. It is closer to what a human employment contract is, with the additional legal structure that constructs require.

Murderbot says yes.

It does not do this because a governor module requires it. It does not do this because it has no other option. It does this because these people are its crew and it has spent the equivalent of years traveling alone through Corporation Rim space to get back to them, and the answer to the question of what it wants has been becoming clearer for three books.

It stays.
